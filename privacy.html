WanderMint Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 22, 2026

WanderMint ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the WanderMint iOS application, how we use it, and your rights to control or delete your data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and unique user identifier managed through Firebase Authentication.
  • Trip Preferences & Messages: Destinations, travel dates, group size, budget level, travel style, interests, special requests, and messages you send regarding your trips.
  • Loyalty Points & Miles Balances: Optional point and mile totals you enter (e.g., credit card, hotel, and airline loyalty programs) solely to help craft tailored award travel recommendations. We never ask for or store your loyalty account passwords, credit card numbers, or financial credentials.
  • Diagnostics & Crash Data: Crash reports and basic app performance diagnostics collected via Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics to fix bugs and keep the app reliable.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and deliver personalized travel itineraries and award booking recommendations.
  • To send optional email notifications when your itinerary is ready or when you opt in to trip updates.
  • To allow you to communicate about trip adjustments inside the app.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve app stability.

3. Data Sharing & Tracking

We never sell your personal information, and we do not track you across third-party apps or websites for advertising. Your data is stored securely on Google Firebase infrastructure (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) and accessed only by you and your WanderMint travel consultant to prepare your requested itinerary.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You may permanently delete your account and all associated data (profile, points balances, trips, and messages) at any time directly inside the WanderMint app by navigating to Profile → Privacy & Security → Delete Account. Account deletion is immediate and irreversible.

5. Security

All network communication between the WanderMint app and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. Access to your records is restricted by Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore security rules.
